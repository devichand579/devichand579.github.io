Drag across your orange cells. Infuse to scatter. Settle to stay.

Win. Hold more ground than either rival when time runs out.

Lose. A rival holds more, or your cells are gone.

You learn. Changing fast fills empty ground after a hit, then costs offspring once the ground is full.

Lehman and Stanley, 2013
