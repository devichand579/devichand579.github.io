Drag the dial. Keep learning high until someone finds the net, then turn it down.

Win. 90% are born knowing the net, with energy left, before generation 100.

Lose. The energy runs out, or generation 100 comes first.

You learn. Learning need not be inherited. It shows selection the rare wiring, and the genes catch up.

Hinton and Nowlan, 1987
